# CIVAJS landing page

This repository contains the public-facing static landing page for CIVAJS.

## Preview

Open `index.html` in a browser or serve the repository root with a static web server.

## Important operational limits

- The intake form is a visual preview only. It does not securely submit applications.
- The newsletter form is not connected to a mailing-list provider.
- Analytics and third-party chat scripts are intentionally disabled until a privacy notice and consent controls are ready.
- The New York address shown is the Consulate General of Canada, a government office and not a CIVAJS business address.
- CIVAJS must independently verify its legal identity, contact details and the authorization status of any person offering paid Canadian immigration advice or representation before publishing service claims.

Do not collect passport numbers, identity documents or CVs through this static page. Do not claim that an application or subscription has been received until a secure backend confirms it.
